# NEET UG 2026 - Complete Syllabus & Exam Guide

## Overview

The NEET (UG) Examination is a pre-medical entrance test for students aspiring to secure admission in undergraduate medical programs at government, private colleges, and international institutions. NEET (UG) stands for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate). It is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) every year.

| Particulars | Details |
|---|---|
| Examination Name | National Eligibility cum Entrance Test |
| Organising Body | National Testing Agency (Since 2019) |
| Official Website(s) | nta.ac.in, neet.nta.nic.in |
| Qualification Eligibility | 12th passed with Physics, Chemistry, Biology |
| Minimum Age Criteria | 17 years |
| Examination Date | May 2026 |
| Examination Mode | Pen and Paper (Hybrid Mode in 2 Phases) |
| Frequency | Once in a year |
| Languages | 13 languages (Hindi, English, Punjabi, Assamese, Bengali, Odia, Gujarati, Marathi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Tamil, Urdu) |

### Courses

- MBBS - Bachelor of Medicine and a Bachelor of Surgery
- BDS - Bachelor of Dental Surgery
- BAMS - Bachelor of Ayurveda, Medicine, and Surgery
- BUMS - Bachelor in Unani Medicine and Surgery
- BHMS - Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery

## Exam Pattern

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Mode of Exam | Hybrid Mode (Conducted in Two Phases) |
| Duration and Timing | 3 hours (2:00 PM to 05:00 PM) |
| Number of Questions | 180 |
| Type of Question | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) |
| Questions per Subject | Physics (45), Chemistry (45), Biology (90) |
| Total Marks | 720 |
| Marking Scheme | Correct: +4 marks, Incorrect: -1 mark, Unanswered: 0 |
| Languages | 13 Languages |

### Marking Scheme Details

- Each question is worth 4 marks
- Correct/most suitable answer: **+4 marks**
- Incorrect option: **-1 mark**
- Unanswered: **0 marks**
- Multiple correct options: +4 marks for any correct choice
- All options correct: +4 marks for all who attempt
- Question dropped/wrong: +4 marks for all candidates

## Reservation Criteria

### 85% State Quota Seats
- Merit List published; seats filled through individual state/UT counselling processes

### 15% All India Quota (AIQ) Seats
- Open to Indian Nationals, NRIs, OCI, PIO, and Foreign Nationals
- Candidates must participate in Online Counselling conducted by MCC

| Category | Reservation % |
|---|---|
| SC | 15% |
| ST | 7.50% |
| OBC-NCL | 27% |
| GEN-EWS | 10% |
| PwD | 5% |

### EWS Eligibility Criteria
- Family annual income not exceeding ₹8,00,000
- Ownership of 5 acres of agricultural land or more
- Residential flat measuring 1000 sq. ft. or more
- Residential plot 100 sq. yards or more in notified municipalities
- Residential plot 200 sq. yards or more in other areas
